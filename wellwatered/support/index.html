Support

Something not working, or a question that isn't answered here? Write to {supportEmail} and we'll get back to you.

My reminders stopped.

WellWatered plans your reminders two weeks ahead and refreshes that plan every time you open the app. If you haven't opened it for more than two weeks, open it once and they start again. If that doesn't help, check that notifications are allowed: in WellWatered, open Settings and look at Reminders. If notifications are turned off on your iPhone, you'll see an Open settings button there.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one, and your plants stay on your phone. An account is only for the optional backup.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your plants, photos and watering history live on your phone, so deleting the app deletes them too. If backup is on, a copy stays in your account, but getting it back onto a phone isn't possible in the app yet. So if you want to keep your plants, please don't delete the app for now.

Can I move my plants to a new phone?

Not yet. With backup on, a copy of your plants is kept in your account, but the app can't load it onto another phone yet.

How do I download or delete my data?

In WellWatered, open Settings and go to Backup. When you're signed in, Download my data saves everything that's backed up as a zip file, and Delete my account and data removes your account and everything backed up. The plants on your phone stay where they are.

Why is the suggested watering interval only a guess?

The suggestions are rough rules of thumb for a pot indoors at room temperature. Light, pot size and the time of year all change how quickly soil dries out. If the soil is still damp when the reminder comes, change the interval in the plant's Edit screen. Think of it as a starting point.

Which languages does the app speak?

English and German. You can switch in Settings, under Language.
