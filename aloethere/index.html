one, not ten

One reminder, only when it matters.

A single nudge in the morning (you pick the time) on days a plant is due. Quiet the rest of the week. If you forget, it reminds you again tomorrow.

the fun part

Watch them grow.

When you take a new photo, the last one shows faintly on top so you can line it up. Over the months you get a little flip book of each plant.

Your plants stay on your phone.

No account needed. If you want a backup, you can make one with just your email, download all of your backed-up data as a file, or delete it whenever you like.

what's next

We're keeping it simple for now. We are working on making AloeThere a bit smarter about your plants over time.
