Privacy

Who is responsible

Lukas Kobler, cabosol.apps@gmail.com

What is stored

Without an account, everything stays on your phone. If you create an account and keep backup on, we store: your plants, your photos, your watering history, your email address, and records of the choices you made (what you agreed to, when, and which version of this text).

Why

Backup: so a copy of your plants, photos and watering history is kept on our server. Getting them back onto a new phone isn't available in the app yet. Turning backup off stops new copies; what is already stored stays until you delete your account.

Improving watering advice (optional, off unless you tick it): we may use your plants, watering history and photos to improve the advice the app gives. For this they are identified only by a random account number, not by your email address. This is not anonymous: the account number still links them to your account. Backup works without it.

Where

Your data is kept in a Supabase project hosted in the EU, in Ireland.

How long

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your data from the server.

Your rights

You can download your data, delete your account and data, and withdraw either choice at any time in Settings. You can also write to cabosol.apps@gmail.com.

Contact

cabosol.apps@gmail.com

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The fonts and images are served from this site itself, so visiting it doesn't send anything to anyone else.
